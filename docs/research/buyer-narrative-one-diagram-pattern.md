# Pattern: one buyer diagram (collapse A–E)

## Source
- Loop Engineering: high-signal surfaces; hide operational depth behind handoff layers.
- Product score dim **simplicity (3.5)**: five mental models + F-IDs on landing raise cognitive load.
- AppSec buyer calls: “what do I install?” beats “explain F186 pressure.”

## Steal for Torii
1. Canonical `docs/brand/BUYER-DIAGRAM.md`: Review + check → Compound → `torii/gate`.
2. Primary phrase on every buyer surface: **stricter and quieter**.
3. Landing/PRODUCT/README lead with one diagram; F-stack under **Advanced** / `docs/research/`.
4. Hermetic `buyer_narrative_check.py` budgets F-counts on primary slices.

## Anti-pattern
